## For parents (the longer notes)

### New zones

- **Zone 6, Gear Works.** Common factors and multiples, the greatest common factor and least common multiple, simplest form for fractions and ratios, and gear trains that turn by their gear ratio. The Conveyor Sync build opens the zone.
- **Zone 7, Crystal Shards.** Fractions (unit fractions, equivalence, comparing, adding, mixed numbers), decimals to hundredths, percents, ratios with the alloy mixer, and numbers below zero on the elevator's depth gauge. Fractions can be written on the chalk slate, numerator over denominator.
- **Zone 8, Power Tower.** Exponents and roots, powers of two and ten, binary and other bases, and scientific notation. *(Details to be filled in when Zone 8 is merged.)*
- **Zone 9, Pattern Grotto.** Sequences that add or multiply, triangular and square numbers, the Fibonacci sequence and how its ratios close in on the golden ratio, Gauss's sum 1 + 2 + ... + n, and proofs without words: children fit two staircases into a rectangle, or two triangles into a square, and pick the fact the picture proves. Answers go on a pattern wall of crystal tiles, on the number pad, on the chalk slate or on the proof board.

### Fib poems

From the Gem Museum, children who know their Fibonacci numbers can open the **Fib Poem Maker**: six lines whose syllables grow 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8. They build each line from about 150 word tiles; a line glows when its syllables fit. Finished poems are saved in the child's own profile and can be read again under "My poems". There is no typing: every word is a tile, and the game only checks the syllable counts, never the words. Poems are stored as word tiles, never as free text or handwriting, and leave the device only inside a progress file a parent exports.

### Scholar mode, part 1

For older children, behind the parent setting: keyboard entry for answers and real on-screen math notation. Explorer mode is unchanged for children who stay in it. *(Details to be filled in when Scholar mode part 1 is merged.)*

### Badges

A new mathematician badge, **Gauss's Staircase**, is earned by mastering Gauss's sum (both skills). It tells how two staircases of gems fit into a rectangle, and why the sum is half of it. The Phase 2 Gauss badge (earned with sums in Zone 2) stays as it was.

### Everything else

- Each new zone has its own backdrop colors, banner and music loop, and Bolt celebrates when every skill of a zone is mastered.
- Progress files from Version 1.0 and every Phase 4 update load into 1.5. The save format went from version 3 to 4 to hold saved poems (an additive change; Spiro approved it).
- Still no network access, no accounts, no ads, no purchases and no new SDK. The data safety answers are unchanged.
