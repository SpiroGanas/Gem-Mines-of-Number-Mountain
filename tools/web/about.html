Gem Mines of Number Mountain

A mining game that teaches number theory to children aged 6 to 12.

Play in your browser

For grown-ups

  • The page loads the game files once. After that, the game makes no network requests of its own.
  • Progress is saved in this browser on this device. Clearing site data erases it.
  • There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Our host (GitHub Pages) keeps ordinary access logs that the game never reads.
  • Handwriting is read on your device and thrown away at once. Without a touch screen the game shows a number pad.
  • Use Chrome on a Chromebook, laptop or tablet. Tap or click the title screen to start the sound.
